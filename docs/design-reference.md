# Design reference

Ted’s visual direction takes high-level inspiration from the warm, optimistic football-club world of Ted Lasso while remaining an original identity.

Primary references:

- [Official Ted Lasso page on Apple TV](https://tv.apple.com/us/show/ted-lasso/umc.cmc.vtoh0mn0xn7t3c643xqonfzy)
- [Official Apple TV Press photography](https://www.apple.com/tv-pr/originals/ted-lasso/photos/)
- [Official episode and production imagery](https://www.apple.com/tv-pr/originals/ted-lasso/episodes-images/)
- [Official trailers](https://www.apple.com/tv-pr/originals/ted-lasso/videos/)

The implemented browser interface is intentionally plain and operational. It uses one system-font size, a white surface, gray rules, blue links and actions, and red only for errors. The direction takes practical inspiration from [SourceHut](https://sourcehut.org/) while Ted Lasso informs the warmth and clarity of the writing rather than decorative imitation.

Do not use official photography, character likenesses, dialogue, the fictional club crest, Apple marks, episode art, or the series logotype. A future redesign may evolve the palette, but it should preserve simplicity, warmth, candor, humility, and forward motion.
