# Design reference

Ted’s visual direction takes high-level inspiration from the warm, optimistic football-club world of Ted Lasso while remaining an original identity.

Primary references:

- [Official Ted Lasso page on Apple TV](https://tv.apple.com/us/show/ted-lasso/umc.cmc.vtoh0mn0xn7t3c643xqonfzy)
- [Official Apple TV Press photography](https://www.apple.com/tv-pr/originals/ted-lasso/photos/)
- [Official episode and production imagery](https://www.apple.com/tv-pr/originals/ted-lasso/episodes-images/)
- [Official trailers](https://www.apple.com/tv-pr/originals/ted-lasso/videos/)

The implemented system uses deep club blue, a narrow red accent, warm paper, optimistic yellow notes, locker-room labels, and a hand-marked tactics board. The product content inside that system is Ted’s own: objectives, plan versions, review dates, training effort, and nutrition guidance.

Do not use official photography, character likenesses, dialogue, the fictional club crest, Apple marks, episode art, or the series logotype. The footer must retain the independent-project statement. A future redesign may evolve the palette, but it should preserve warmth, candor, humility, and forward motion.
