# Exercise catalog

Every movement used by the seeded workout templates has a versioned local image and a direct video link. The movement configuration, including sets, repetitions, rest, and safety guidance, lives with the named workout template in `priv/repo/seeds.exs`.

## Equipment coverage

| Equipment | Movements |
| --- | --- |
| Barbell | Back squat, bench press, bent-over row, deadlift, overhead press |
| Dumbbell and bodyweight | Goblet squat, push-up, single-arm row, hip hinge, pike push-up, split squat, rear-foot-elevated split squat, pull-up |
| Cable | Lat pulldown, seated cable row, triceps pressdown, biceps curl |
| Selectorized machine | Chest press, leg press, leg extension, prone leg curl, shoulder press, hip abduction, hip adduction |

## Facility inventory boundary

Public material for [aGYM at Frankfurter Allee](https://agym.co/de?hasLoaded=true) describes free weights, cable equipment, a functional area, cardio equipment, and a ladies area. It does not publish a complete machine-by-machine inventory. The machine entries above use generic equipment names, so they can be matched to the installed equipment during a first visit. Do not treat the catalog as confirmation of a particular model being available.
